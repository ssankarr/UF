Can Uloans Finance help me with bridging finance?

Yes. Bridging loans involve timing risk — you're essentially carrying two properties for a period. We help you understand peak debt, what happens if your existing property takes longer to sell, and which lenders offer the most flexible bridging terms.

When should I refinance my mortgage?

Usually when your rate hasn't been reviewed in two or more years, or when your property has grown in value and you want to access equity. A quick comparison often reveals savings of $200–$400 a month. The costs of switching are real but usually recovered within 12–18 months.

How much does refinancing cost?

Discharge fees, valuation costs, and sometimes break costs if you're on a fixed rate. These vary by lender. We run the numbers before recommending a switch — if the savings don't justify it, we'll tell you.

What documents will I need to apply for a home loan?

For most applications: two forms of ID, your last two payslips, three months of bank statements, and details of any existing debts or assets. Self-employed applicants typically need two years of tax returns and notices of assessment, and sometimes business financials. We'll give you a specific list for your situation before you start gathering anything.

Do you support first home buyers?

Yes. We help first-home buyers understand the First Home Owner Grant, stamp duty concessions, and the Home Guarantee Scheme (where 5% deposit is enough). We also explain how much genuine savings you need and what lenders actually look at.

Can you assist self-employed clients?

Yes, and this is one of our more common client types. ABN holders, sole traders, company directors, and SMSF trustees all face a different assessment process than PAYG employees. We know which lenders use full income verification and which use alternative documentation, and we match you to the right one.

What exactly is an offset account?

An offset account sits alongside your loan. The balance in it reduces the principal amount interest is calculated on. So if you owe $500,000 and have $50,000 in offset, you only pay interest on $450,000. It doesn't reduce your loan balance, but it reduces your interest bill and shortens your loan term if you keep adding to it.

Why are pre-approvals important?

Pre-approvals give you a confirmed borrowing limit before you make an offer. They prevent you from bidding on properties you can't actually finance, and they give vendors confidence you're a serious buyer. They typically last 90 days. We recommend having one before you start inspecting seriously.
