# Uloans Finance

Site type: static marketing website for a mortgage broker based in Pakenham, Victoria.
Primary audience: home buyers, refinancers, first-home buyers, self-employed borrowers, investors, and local South-East Melbourne prospects.
Primary conversion goals: loan health check, callback request, calculator usage, direct contact.

## Primary pages

- /index.html
  - Homepage
  - Main overview of services, trust signals, calculator, testimonials, and lead capture

- /mortgage-broker-pakenham.html
  - Local SEO landing page for mortgage broker searches in Pakenham
  - Focus on suburb intent, local lending support, and conversion pathways

- /about.html
  - Founder and company background
  - Trust, values, service approach, lender access, and expertise

- /calculators.html
  - Loan repayment calculator page
  - Payment estimates for monthly, fortnightly, and weekly scenarios

- /faq.html
  - Frequently asked questions
  - Covers refinance, documents, first-home buyers, offset accounts, and lending scenarios

- /contact.html
  - Contact and enquiry page
  - Phone, email, business hours, WhatsApp QR, and callback/contact form

- /latest-updates.html
  - Updates and insight content page
  - Intended for news, rate commentary, and ongoing SEO content
