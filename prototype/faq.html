Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for common lending questions

This FAQ page keeps the subjects visible on the live site, but presents them in a more readable and mobile-friendly format.

Can Uloans Finance help me with bridging finance?

Yes. Uloans can guide borrowers through more complex structures such as bridging finance where timing, property sale assumptions, and servicing strategy matter.

When should I refinance my mortgage?

Refinancing can make sense when rates are uncompetitive, your loan features no longer suit your needs, or you want to consolidate debt or access equity.

How much does refinancing cost?

Costs vary by lender and loan type. A broker can compare break costs, discharge fees, valuation costs, and any longer-term savings before you switch.

What documents will I need to apply for a home loan?

Most applications need ID, income evidence, bank statements, liabilities, and asset details. Self-employed borrowers usually need extra tax and business documents.

Do you support first home buyers?

Yes. Uloans supports first home buyers with grants, guarantees, deposit strategies, and lender comparisons suited to first-entry borrowers.

Can you assist self-employed clients?

Yes. This is one of the key specialist areas highlighted on the live site, including company, SMSF, and self-employed lending scenarios.

What exactly is an offset account?

An offset account is a linked transaction account that reduces the interest charged on your loan by offsetting the balance against the loan principal.

Why are pre-approvals important?

Pre-approvals help set a realistic purchase range, strengthen your buying confidence, and reduce surprises later in the property process.
